Wildbound alpha support

Contact wildboundexplore@gmail.com for installation, access or privacy questions.

Wildbound is an early wildlife exploration game. Go outside, discover wildlife, and see what happens. It is unfinished, so if something is confusing, that is useful feedback.

When identification is wrong or unavailable

Wildbound currently supports a small regional selection of wildlife for the Greater Boston / Eastern Massachusetts pilot, plus domestic Dog and Cat. It is not a complete regional field guide. AI suggestions may be wrong or unavailable, including for animals Wildbound supports.

Choose “Choose a species I recognize” if you know the animal, or “I'm not sure” / save for review if you do not. Manual choice is your identification, not an AI result or scientific verification. External identification is optional and explained before you send a photo.

Use photos you own or have permission to process. Avoid identifiable photos of people without appropriate permission. Observe animals safely and respectfully.

Your invited testing access

Identification access is limited to your invitation's testing period and attempt allowance. It may also be paused for safety or service availability. Once it ends, you can still use locally saved Wildbook, Sanctuary and observation data while the installed build remains usable; access expiry does not erase that data. No purchase or subscription is required.

TestFlight builds themselves expire, normally up to 90 days after upload, and may be withdrawn earlier. An expired build will need an update to open. See Apple's TestFlight guide. Do not rely on the alpha as the only copy of important photos.

Something isn't working

Send the app version, a short description, visible error text and whether you were offline. You can also use TestFlight's existing beta feedback tools. Do not send passwords, invitation codes, API keys, private photographs or precise wildlife locations.

Do not uninstall just to troubleshoot. Uninstalling may permanently lose locally saved photos and progress. Contact us first; Wildbound has no cloud restore service for this alpha.

If an invitation or access credential is compromised, contact us to revoke it. Replacing access does not reset the original allowance.

For access/deletion requests and details of external processing, read the privacy notice.
