Wildbound invited-alpha privacy notice

Effective date: October 8, 2026.

Wildbound is an early wildlife exploration game operated by its founder. This alpha is for individually invited adults aged 18 or older. For privacy, access or deletion requests, contact wildboundexplore@gmail.com.

Your field journal stays on your device

Your saved photos, observations, notes, Wildbook, quests and Sanctuary progress are stored on your iPhone. This alpha has no Wildbound cloud account or gameplay synchronization. Wildbound cannot remotely retrieve or erase those local records. Device backups may contain app data depending on your Apple settings; keep your own originals of important photographs.

Ending identification access does not erase your local journal. It remains usable while the app is installed and the build can run. TestFlight builds have their own expiry, after which an updated build is needed. Uninstalling can permanently lose local app data; do not use it as a troubleshooting step without understanding that loss.

Sending a photo is your choice

Before external identification, Wildbound explains the processing and asks you to choose whether to send. A resized JPEG copy with original metadata removed passes through Wildbound's Cloudflare service to OpenAI for animal-identification suggestions. We do not include precise GPS, field notes, original EXIF, camera serial numbers or unrelated user/device identifiers in that provider request.

Cloudflare handles your connection and can process IP addresses and other network/security information. OpenAI receives the image and Wildbound's identification instructions through the service. Wildbound does not forward your invitation credential or device connection headers to OpenAI. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Wildbound's Worker processes the image for the request without saving the raw image to its database or an image store. Application logging of raw images is disabled. This does not mean infrastructure providers retain no information.

Wildbound has disabled OpenAI's optional sharing of API inputs/outputs, evaluation/fine-tuning data and platform model feedback, as checked on October 8, 2026. OpenAI says API content is not used to train its models unless the account opts in. Default abuse-monitoring retention is up to 30 days, with policy exceptions for longer retention. Wildbound does not claim Zero Data Retention. See OpenAI's API data controls and retention information.

You can instead save an observation for review or choose a species you recognize. AI may be wrong or unavailable. Catalog support, an AI suggestion, a player's choice and scientific verification are different things. Camera capture alone does not prove that an animal was physically present.

Access and minimal operational records

Individual invited access uses an opaque credential held in the device's secure storage. The service uses hashes and pseudonymous tester references, access expiry and revocation status, request IDs, timestamps, outcome codes, latency and token/cost records to enforce access and spending limits. Pseudonymous does not mean anonymous: the founder may connect a tester reference to private invitation administration. These records do not contain the raw photo or field notes.

Revoking access prevents new requests, but cannot recall a request already in progress. It does not delete existing spending history or restore used allowances.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Local journal and photos: remain on your device until removed with app data or otherwise lost. The alpha has no individual-photo deletion or remote journal-erasure service. Apple backups are separate.
  • Invitation/contact details and the private testing log: used for invitations, adult-eligibility confirmation, support and the agreed follow-up. Our practice: review them within 30 days after your testing period; delete contact links and identifiable notes within 90 days after it ends, unless you separately agree to ongoing contact or a specific unresolved request, security incident or legal duty requires retention. Keep only de-identified product findings after that. No birth dates or identity documents are requested for this cohort.
  • Support/privacy correspondence and founder-held diagnostic copies: practice: remove or de-identify them within 90 days after the issue is resolved. If retention is still needed for a specific dispute, security issue or applicable obligation, retain only what is necessary and review it when that issue ends.
  • Minimal access, reservation and usage records: the current service keeps append-only history throughout this pilot's lifetime to prevent quota resets, enforce the shared spending ceiling and reconcile costs. It has no automatic purge or individual deletion mechanism for that history. At service retirement, assess what is still needed for unresolved accounting/security or applicable legal obligations; there is no promised deletion date. Do not read this technical retention design as a claim that all such records are legally exempt from deletion.
  • Provider-controlled records: OpenAI, Cloudflare, Apple, GitHub and the email provider apply their own policies. Wildbound cannot promise to erase their independently retained security records or backups.

Access, correction or deletion requests

Email wildboundexplore@gmail.com, preferably from your invitation/contact address, and describe the request. We will use the minimum information needed to connect it to your records. Do not send passwords, invitation codes, API keys, government ID, private photos or precise wildlife locations.

The founder can review records held by Wildbound, provide an appropriate copy of those records, correct contact information, remove nonessential founder-held contact/feedback records and stop identification access. There is no self-service export or deletion portal. We will explain what can be removed, what remains and why, and address applicable privacy rights. Local-only data cannot be fetched or remotely deleted by the founder. A request to stop testing is not automatically a deletion request; uninstalling does not delete service or provider records.

If a request requires changes to the protected accounting system or action by a provider, it needs individual review. We will not promise deletion that has not been performed. Provider privacy links below explain their own request channels.

Apple TestFlight, support email and this website

Apple automatically collects TestFlight usage and crash information and shares it with the app developer; this can include contact, device and installation details. This collection cannot be opted out of while using TestFlight. Feedback you choose to send is additional. Apple says it retains beta feedback for one year and may keep crash/usage data until bugs are resolved. See TestFlight & Privacy. We use these records for alpha support and improvement, not advertising, and do not share Apple's tester reports with third parties.

Messages sent to our support address are processed through Gmail. See Google's privacy policy.

This privacy/support site uses GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, tracking scripts, forms or advertising cookies. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security; see GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement. Hosting a static page is not a promise of anonymous browsing.

Providers may process information in other countries under their policies. The founder's contact-record removal does not override their retention.

Respect people and wildlife

Use photographs you own or have permission to process. Wildbound is for animal observation, not identifying people. Avoid submitting identifiable photographs of people without their appropriate permission; do not include sensitive personal information.

Observe wildlife at a respectful distance. Do not feed, touch, corner or disturb animals, approach nests or dens, trespass or take unsafe photographs. Do not disclose sensitive wildlife locations.

No advertising or behavioral tracking

This alpha has no advertising, monetization, social-sharing service, in-app feedback backend or analytics SDK. Wildbound does not sell your private observations or precise user/wildlife locations. Apple's beta diagnostics and the service's minimal operational records described above still apply.

We will update this notice and the relevant disclosure before materially changing the described processing.
